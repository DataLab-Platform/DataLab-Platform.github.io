Workspace capsules#

A capsule (.dlcapsule file) holds a workspace and a description of the provenance of its processing (see Provenance of results), so that someone else can check it and replay it, with DataLab or with DataLab-Web. Capsules are created, read and checked by DataLab-Capsule: opening or inspecting a capsule never executes anything it contains.

Capsules are handled from File > Workspace capsule…, a dialog with the same tabs as in DataLab-Web. Three results are always shown apart:

  • the integrity of a capsule, computed from its files (nothing is recomputed);

  • the supplied documents (verification reports and reviews): they are checked against the capsule, but their verdicts are what their authors claim;

  • the local replay of an activity of the open workspace, recomputed by DataLab and compared with its stored result.

Export#

Exports the current workspace as a capsule:

  • Name and Description of the capsule;

  • Revision of …: shown when the workspace was opened from a capsule, also after it was saved as a workspace file and opened again. When checked, the new capsule names that capsule as its parent revision. The attachments of the parent are not copied: the dialog lists them, so that they can be selected again if they still apply;

  • Reference the workspace instead of including it: the capsule then holds the address (absolute IRI) where the workspace will be published, with its size and SHA-256, and the workspace is saved as a separate HDF5 file, to be published at that address;

  • Attachments: light files (protocol, notes, small tables, 16 MiB at most each) declared in the capsule with their size and fingerprint. They are never opened or executed.

Export capsule… asks where to save the capsule (and, for a referenced workspace, the workspace file).

Warning

Review the data, metadata, titles, trials, timestamps and attachments before sharing. Nothing is anonymized or redacted automatically. Deleting an object does not guarantee that its traces disappear from the provenance record.

The export carries the current workspace, its record and the chosen attachments, not every external resource used upstream. It does not automatically select the minimal analysis needed for one result: trials and intermediate objects may travel with it. Copying only a result to another workspace does not make that workspace a complete verifiable analysis. Prepare a reduced exchange by deliberately keeping or rebuilding the useful inputs and checking what remains verifiable, not by editing the record by hand.

The software licence does not license the data. The manifest contains a workspace licence declaration; when none is supplied, it uses the neutral statement “No licence was specified for this workspace.”

Inspect#

Choose a capsule… checks a capsule without opening it and shows:

  • its summary: name, description, fingerprint, parent revision, whether the workspace is included or referenced, licence, and its activities (which ones can be replayed);

  • its Attachments, which are checked against the manifest and saved only when you click Save…;

  • its Integrity: archive, manifest, workspace and stored states. For a referenced workspace, Select the referenced workspace… supplies the file, which must match the size and SHA-256 declared in the capsule;

  • the Supplied documents added with Add a verification report… and Add a review…, each checked against the capsule.

Write a review creates a review of this revision: a separate JSON file (reviewer, statement, optional verdict endorsed, disputed or comment, cited verification reports). The capsule itself is not changed.

Open as workspace replaces the current workspace by the workspace of the capsule (after confirmation when the workspace is not empty). It is available only for an intact capsule. A capsule exported later from this workspace is proposed as a revision of it. Opening a capsule is recorded in the History panel.

For a referenced capsule, obtain the HDF5 file separately: its address is not downloaded automatically, and its size and fingerprint must match. A registry does not deliver that workspace on your behalf.

Local replay#

Lists the recorded activities of the open workspace. Replay recomputes an activity separately and compares every result with the stored one: Exact, Within tolerance, Different or Not verified (with the reason). The workspace is not changed. Save report… saves the verification report as a JSON file, to be supplied with the capsule or cited by a review.

Revisions#

Lists the revisions exported, inspected or retrieved from a registry on this computer (with their parent revision), and compares any two of them: scientific, presentation, storage and integrity, environment and attachment changes, with a scientific conclusion that never claims equality beyond what the capsules record. The list is kept in the DataLab configuration folder (100 revisions at most); it is a convenience, never a source of truth: the capsule files are.

A capsule is immutable and names at most one direct parent. If you open A and export B, the next export still proposes A as its parent, not B; saving and reopening the workspace preserves that origin. Inspecting another file does not change it. Adding a workspace keeps the current record and parent without merging provenance (see Appending a workspace).

Two exports can have different fingerprints without a new calculation: HDF5 storage, dates or presentation can change. none_detected means no scientific change detected in the recorded activities and states, not equality of everything outside the record. Changing an attached protocol is listed under attachments; the reader, not the comparison, judges its scientific importance. There is no automatic merge of concurrent work, collaborative editing or conflict resolution.

Registry#

A registry (optional, served by DataLab-Capsule) publishes capsule manifests, their attachments and reviews, never workspaces. Everything it returns is checked by DataLab.

  • Registry address and Publishing token: the address is saved in the configuration; the token is kept for the current session only;

  • List revisions lists the published revisions; Retrieve checks a revision and its reviews, adds it to the Revisions tab and offers its stored attachments, checked against the manifest;

  • once a registry is set, the Inspect tab offers Publish to the registry (manifest, attachments and valid supplied reviews) and Publish the review.

The reference registry has no accounts, strong author identity, quotas, rate limiting, delete API or fine-grained permissions. Its publishing token is not proof of scientific authorship. See the existing deployment and trust guidance. Reviews and reports target one revision; a checked author’s assertion does not replace local replay or implement a complete approval workflow.

From scripts#

Capsules can be exported and opened from scripts and macros (see Remote controlling (XML-RPC)), with export_capsule() and open_capsule():

fingerprint = proxy.export_capsule(
    "chain.dlcapsule", name="chain", attachments=["protocol.txt"]
)
proxy.open_capsule("chain.dlcapsule")

The workspace-capsule command line of DataLab-Capsule also validates, inspects, compares, verifies and publishes capsules.

Its verify command performs no Sigima calculation. Without h5py, workspace checking is limited to size and SHA-256 (integrity_only), not individual HDF5 states. A referenced workspace not supplied further limits the checks.

Limits#

DataLab checks the size of a capsule, and of the workspace it references, before reading it, and never writes a capsule it would refuse to open. These limits are those of DataLab-Capsule (a workspace of 4 GiB at most): they are accepted sizes, not a tested capacity.

What was measured, on a laptop of 2026 (32 GiB of RAM):

  • inspecting or opening a 32 MiB capsule from the dialog takes 0.2 to 0.5 s, during which DataLab does not respond (wait cursor); exporting it takes 0.25 s. Larger capsules take longer, roughly in proportion; the library verifies a 234 MiB capsule in less than a second;

  • opening a capsule needs about its size in memory, in addition to the workspace it replaces;

  • recording the provenance of a processing costs the fingerprints of its inputs and results: nothing measurable for a small signal, +29 ms for a 2048×2048 image filtered in 450 ms;

  • consulting the provenance of a result takes less than a millisecond, even with 1000 recorded steps; listing 1000 steps in Local replay takes about half a second.

Each processing gets slightly slower as a panel holds more objects, with or without provenance (about 0.1 s in an empty panel, 0.3 s with 1000 objects). DataLab-Web limits capsules to 256 MiB.

Accepted file policies, not guaranteed capacities#

Consumer

Workspace / manifest

Desktop and library

4 GiB / 64 MiB; the archive has a separate total-size limit

Web

256 MiB / 16 MiB; archive also limited to 256 MiB

Reference registry

No workspace; manifest 8 MiB, review 1 MiB

Each capsule accepts at most 8 attachments of 16 MiB each. Memory can hold several representations of the archive and HDF5 while exporting; a valid file may exceed another edition’s policy. The ledger also has JSON volume and structural bounds, even without a fixed activity-count limit. View truncation does not remove records. See the technical limits and measured capacity.

Local replay verifies one activity on copies; it does not reconstruct an entire chain, restore all missing states or provide a workflow for new datasets. Environment versions are described, not reinstalled: a capsule is not an OS with all its dependencies, and a successful comparison is not universal reproducibility between machines.

Fingerprints detect changes but do not authenticate authors; capsules and reviews are unsigned and their fingerprints can be recomputed after editing. Import never runs received macros, notebooks, attachments or Python names; explicit replay uses locally registered, qualified operations. The prescribed profile accepts native supported workspaces, not arbitrary ZIP/HDF5 files: ZIP entries are uncompressed (independent of internal HDF5 compression), and links and encrypted archives are refused. Format checks do not replace a sandbox: HDF5 parsing and dependencies remain trusted code on the Desktop.

Profile 0.1 is experimental and unknown versions are refused. RO-Crate/PROV metadata do not guarantee that any third-party reader can restore or replay DataLab. See the common rules and security analysis. Desktop and the CLI can work offline once installed; the Web downloads its runtime at startup and some optional packages on first use. Computations remain local; neither a registry nor attachments certify the physical acquisition.
