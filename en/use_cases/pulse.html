DataLab for transient and pulsed signals#

The problem#

Pulsed lasers, pulse generators, amplifiers, detectors and accelerators are characterized from transient signals recorded by oscilloscopes and digitizers, often over hundreds of shots: which shots are valid, what the representative pulse looks like, how much the timing jitters and the amplitude drifts, how fast a system responds to a step, what the delay between two channels is. Oscilloscope measurements describe one shot at a time, and campaign scripts must handle baselines, polarities, invalid shots and alignment before giving any number.

What DataLab does#

DataLab analyzes transient signals out of the box:

  • Analysis > Extract pulse features… gives the amplitude, rise and fall times, FWHM and timing of step and square pulses (see Analysis features on Signals),

  • Processing > Stability analysis gives the Allan, Hadamard and total variances and the Allan and time deviations of a time series (see Processing Signals).

The Pulse & Transient Characterization plugin adds complete methods for series of shots:

  • pulse campaign: per-shot features and quality flags, raw and aligned mean pulses,

  • shot-to-shot stability: timing jitter, drifts, amplitude and energy stability, Allan deviation, regime changes,

  • step response: rise time, overshoot, settling time, ringing and bandwidth,

  • delay between two channels, with relative and common-mode jitter,

  • pulse-height spectrum: energy calibration and resolution of a detector,

  • an oscilloscope simulator, with a live view, which acquires laser pulses, amplifier steps or delayed pulse pairs ready for the methods.

Results are added to the workspace as signals and metrics tables, and the methods and their examples are presented in the Applications panel.

Status#

The plugin is an official plugin of the DataLab plugin catalog, for DataLab Desktop and DataLab-Web, at the Alpha stage: its methods are validated on synthetic data with known truth, and do not claim compliance with a standard.

Try it#

  • No install: open DataLab-Web with a 500-shot campaign and the pulse campaign method selected in your browser – your data never leaves your machine.

  • In DataLab Desktop: install the plugin from the Catalog tab of Plugins > Configure plugins… (see Catalog tab), then click its tile on the Welcome page.

  • Plugin documentation: getting started and methods.

  • Or install the desktop application.
